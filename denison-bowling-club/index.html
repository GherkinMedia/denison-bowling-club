Community bowls on the Turquoise Coast

A community club with the coast on our doorstep

A not-for-profit club for social and competitive bowls, barefoot bowls, functions and good company.

Denison Bowling Club is a not-for-profit sports and social club in Port Denison, Western Australia. We offer competitive and social bowls, barefoot bowls, functions and a welcoming place to meet for members, local families, visitors and community groups.
